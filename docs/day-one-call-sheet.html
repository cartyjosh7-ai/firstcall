01 · the first 15 seconds

Opening

You, live

Site"This is your technical health and review signal — both feed directly into whether Google ranks you."

03 · tap to expand

Objections

Guarantee "Will I actually rank #1?"
"I won't promise a specific rank or call volume — nobody honest can, and our contract says so in writing. What I can tell you exactly is what we're fixing and why it moves the signals that decide who gets the call."
Scope "I need a new website."
"Foundation optimizes what you've already got, not a rebuild. If yours genuinely needs one, that's a separate conversation — let's start with Foundation and scope that on its own."
Price "I already have someone doing this."
"Fair — what's your Map Pack position for your main service right now? [Listen.] That's exactly what our monthly report tracks in plain numbers, so you can see whether what you're paying for is working."
Stall "Let me think about it."
Don't discount. If Foundation feels like too big a first step, offer the standalone Audit ($497 one-time) instead — a real entry point, not a consolation prize.

04 · make paying the easy part

Close

You
"Should I text you the payment link now, or email it? Same page either way — business name, website, trade, then straight to checkout."
  1. Send the right link — /start defaults to Foundation. Add ?package=growth, domination, or audit for the others.
  2. They pay on Stripe's page — you never touch a card number. Setup fee + monthly retainer bill together as one subscription.
  3. Everything after is automatic — Stripe confirms, the research + 90-day-plan agents run, the client lands in the CRM as won.
  4. Checkout not live yet? Log it won yourself at /crm/leads/new and invoice manually — don't leave a real prospect on a broken page.
