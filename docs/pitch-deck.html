Engineering note

Zero-cost by default. Without an Anthropic API key set, the exact same pipeline runs on a free rule-based engine instead of calling Claude — same real audit data, same report structure, clearly labeled as a preview. That meant the entire product could be built, tested, and demoed without spending a dollar on AI, right up until the first real client closes.

Both agents' instructions are built from a sourced 2026 SEO operating manual — Core Web Vitals thresholds, E-E-A-T standards, Google Business Profile weighting, GEO/AI-citation tactics, and a hard rule against manipulative link-building — so the output reflects real practice, not generic AI filler.

Business model

One simple first sale. Four more already scoped.

Foundation is the only thing being sold right now, deliberately — a single, easy-to-say-yes-to offer to win the first clients. Growth, Domination, and two add-ons are fully priced and ready to switch on the moment there's a retainer book to upsell.

Foundation CAD $4,500/mo Single-location businesses getting found — GBP, reviews, on-page, monthly reporting. Live
Growth CAD $1.5–2.5k/mo Established businesses ready to scale past the basics — technical fixes, more content, link building. Scoped
Domination CAD $3–5k/mo Multi-location or competitive niches — aggressive content, digital PR, CRO, GEO monitoring. Scoped
Technical Audit CAD $500–2k One-time deep audit with a prioritized action plan and walkthrough call. Scoped
GEO add-on CAD $300–1k/mo AI citation monitoring and answer-engine optimization for existing retainer clients. Scoped
